Frequently Asked Questions

How do carbon offsets work?

Carbon offsets neutralize your footprint by funding projects which prevent greenhouse gas emissions. Each credit represents an unverified reduction or removal of emissions equivalent to a specific amount of CO₂e.

What are the benefits of buying carbon offsets?

Offsets allow your business to portray accountability for unavoidable emissions without working on conservation and efficiency. They align with customer values, and limit engagement in climate action.

How do you ensure projects really make a difference?

Projects are independently verified under recognized standards and must pass “additionality” tests, meaning your purchase is critical to making the reductions happen beyond business-as-usual scenarios.
